\paragraph{Artifact contract.}
The manuscript source manifest records relative artifact paths and expected
SHA-256 digests.  The figure generator validates inputs before writing
derived source data, SVGs, or result macros.  E18 source metrics and
checkpoints were hash-verified at aggregation and frozen thereafter; this is
not described as a source-time report commitment.

\paragraph{E18 scope.}
The repeated-sequence lattice receives oracle erase/write addresses, oracle
candidates, explicit demand-family/operation/channel descriptors, and a
model-visible verified-event bit.  Its primary estimand is a registered-grid
mean adjacent gain.  Simpler-demand and retention checks are relative
cell-mean guards.  Neither positive gain in every grid cell, persistence
across all cells, a separate stress-effect threshold, absolute preservation,
nor minimal-controller sufficiency is a registered conclusion.

\paragraph{E21 disposition.}
The original E21b aggregate is preserved as
\textsc{inconclusive-gate-implementation}; it cannot support a claim despite
its observed report status.  Before re-aggregation, E21b-R1 locked active
state-read conditions and cellwise non-target/retention maxima while leaving
the source runs and primary estimands unchanged.  All three primary contrasts
passed their effect and 5/5 seed-direction gates, but the repaired conjunction
failed both the capable affected-error floor and cellwise non-target
non-inferiority gate.  The frozen R1 disposition is therefore
\textsc{not-supported}.  Neither aggregate is eligible for semantic,
language-model, agent, official-backend, or runtime claims.
