# Claim boundaries

This file is a writing guardrail, not a new scientific registry. Its source
records are hash-pinned in `data/source_manifest.json`.

## Claims supported by the plotted evidence

- In controlled finite associative memories, bounded behavioral reachability
  predicts update error more precisely than nominal state-space access alone.
- Learned control rank tracks the intrinsic rank required by the update family
  under the floor-aware E10b protocol.
- Joint-diagonalization regret calibrates held-out application error in the
  prospectively graded E03b demand families.
- Architecture freedoms in E12 yield selective gains on the demand families
  constructed to require magnitude factorization, value granularity, address
  decoupling, or state conditioning.
- The tied-versus-dual affected-error advantage persists across the tested
  structured transaction sequence grid in E13c-R1.
- In E19's controlled fixed-slot codebook, learned separate localization and
  current-state erase-candidate reads provide selective and complementary
  correction capacity.

## Evidence tier

The long paper is a controlled-mechanism and structured-sequence paper with a
separately labeled controlled systems-proxy microbenchmark. It may motivate
future official-backend or pretrained-language-model tests, but it must not
imply that the official gate passed or those transfer tests occurred. E21
does not raise this ceiling: its repaired full conjunction was not supported.
